Visiting card — 5 directions

Five front-and-back designs to pick from, all at the true card size. Phone, full address, website and the WhatsApp number are dummy placeholders — everything else (name, MS · MBBS, specialty, hospital, hours, email) is final. Every card carries an NFC-tap and a QR that both open WhatsApp with “Hi” pre-filled. Pick a direction and I’ll finalise it with the real details for print + NFC.

Print & production spec

Hand this to the printer. All five designs use the same dimensions.

NFC tap → WhatsApp “Hi”

One link does three jobs — the NFC chip, the QR code, and any web button all point to the same WhatsApp deep link, so a tap or a scan opens a chat with “Hi” pre-filled and the patient just hits send. Program the NTAG213 with a single NDEF URI record (a Text record won’t auto-open). Keep metallic foil away from the chip — it detunes the antenna.

https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi

Format: wa.me/91<10-digit number>?text=Hi — no +, no spaces. Replace the dummy number before printing.

Before printing — checklist

  • • Replace dummy phone, full street address, website, and the WhatsApp number (inside the wa.me link).
  • • Tap-test the NFC and scan-test the QR on an Android + an iPhone — confirm WhatsApp opens with “Hi”.
  • • Order a 5–10 card NFC pilot before the full run.
  • • Approve colour off a printed proof on the actual stock, not a screen.
  • • Keep copy purely informational (NMC) — no superlatives, guarantees, or testimonials.
