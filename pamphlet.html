Clinic pamphlet — 5 directions

Five A5 flyers to announce the Obstetrics & Gynaecology service at Shree KC Dada Memorial Hospital, for local distribution around Agra. Designed as a hospital service announcement (factual, NMC-safe — no superlatives, guarantees, or testimonials). Phone, full address and the WhatsApp number are dummy placeholders. Each carries a WhatsApp QR that opens a chat with “Hi”. Mix and match — tell me which elements to keep and I’ll produce the final.

Print & compliance spec

A5 flyer for local distribution. Hand this to the printer.

NMC — keep it factual ✓

  • • Hospital + department, doctor name & real qualifications.
  • • Factual service list, address, hours, contact.
  • • Neutral, reassuring language; tasteful non-clinical motifs.

Never ✕

  • • Superlatives (“best”, “painless”), guarantees, testimonials.
  • • Discounts/free-camp bait, comparisons, before/after images.
  • • Any sex-determination wording (PCPNDT Act) — never.

Hindi: set Devanagari in a Unicode font (Noto Sans/Serif Devanagari — used here), never legacy Kruti Dev; outline/embed fonts in the print PDF and have a native speaker proof the laid-out artwork. Keep phone numbers in Western digits.
