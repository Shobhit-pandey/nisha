About

About

Dr. Nisha is an Obstetrics & Gynaecology physician (MS, MBBS), currently a Senior Resident at ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Faridabad. Her training spans the labour room, operation theatre, OPD, emergency and inpatient wards at busy government teaching hospitals.

Her clinical focus is high-risk obstetrics and labour-room care, alongside a broad range of gynaecology and family-planning procedures. She completed her MS at Pt. B.D. Sharma PGIMS, Rohtak, with a thesis on fetal kidney length as a parameter for gestational-age estimation.

She also teaches MBBS undergraduates, takes part in departmental drills and audits, and is a member of FOGSI. She values careful, evidence-based, patient-centred care and clear communication.
