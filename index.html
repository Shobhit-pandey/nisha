About

Dr. Nisha is a Consultant Obstetrician & Gynaecologist (MS, MBBS) practising at Shree KC Dada Memorial Hospital in Agra. After several years in busy government teaching hospitals, she now cares for women through pregnancy, birth, and gynaecological health closer to home.

Her training spans the labour room, operation theatre, OPD, emergency and inpatient wards — most recently as Senior Resident at ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Faridabad, and through her MS residency at Pt. B.D. Sharma PGIMS, Rohtak (thesis on fetal kidney length as a parameter for gestational-age estimation). Her clinical focus is high-risk obstetrics and labour-room care, alongside a broad range of gynaecology and family-planning procedures.

She has taught MBBS undergraduates, taken part in departmental drills and audits, and is a member of FOGSI. She values careful, evidence-based, patient-centred care and clear communication.
